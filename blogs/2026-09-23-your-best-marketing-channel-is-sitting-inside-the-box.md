Most e-commerce brands will never meet their customers. Unlike traditional retail, the relationship lives almost entirely through screen—where people discover products through ads and social media, explore through influencers, browse websites, read marketing emails, and then check out without ever speaking to someone from sales. The first physical connection from the brand arrives days later as a package.
